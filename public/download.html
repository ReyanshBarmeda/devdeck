Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about downloading or running DevDeck? Find answers below.

Can I use DevDeck offline without an internet connection? ↓

Yes! DevDeck is built with an offline-first architecture. All port scans, project directories, bookmarks, and snippet vaults work locally on your computer without sending telemetry or requiring an internet connection.

How does Google & Gmail authentication work? ↓

DevDeck integrates with Firebase Authentication. When you sign in with your Google or Gmail account (such as reyanshecom@gmail.com), your custom project cards and settings are securely backed up to Cloud Firestore and synchronized across devices.

Is there an automated updater built into DevDeck Desktop? ↓

Yes, DevDeck checks for stable releases at startup and notifies you of new feature drops and security updates with one-click seamless background updates.
